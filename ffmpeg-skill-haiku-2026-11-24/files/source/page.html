Day pack checklist

1

Packing a day pack is easier when you start with the weather.

2

Check the forecast first, because rain changes almost everything you carry.

3

Next, choose a pack that fits your back and sits high on your hips.

4

Put the heavy items close to your spine, so the weight does not pull you backward.

5

Water goes in next, and one liter for every two hours of walking is a good rule.

6

Then add food, and pack more than you think you will eat.

7

A light rain jacket belongs near the top, where your hand can find it quickly.

8

Keep a small first aid kit in a side pocket, with bandages and tape.

9

Your phone and a paper map go in the lid pocket, away from the water.

10

A hat and sunscreen take almost no space, and they save a long afternoon.

11

Before you leave, lift the pack and walk across the room with it.

12

If it feels heavy now, it will feel much heavier after five miles.

13

Take out anything you will not use, and close every zipper.

14

Then you are ready to walk out the door.
