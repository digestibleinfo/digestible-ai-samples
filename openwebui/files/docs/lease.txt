RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
This agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Mr Sipho Dlamini (the Landlord) and Ms Naledi Mokoena (the Tenant).
The Landlord lets to the Tenant Flat 12, Acacia Court, 14 Acacia Road, Sandton, for a period of 12 months.
The monthly rental is R 9 800,00, payable on or before the 1st of each month. A deposit of R 19 600,00 is payable on signature
and will be refunded within 14 days of the end of the lease, less any damages.
No pets may be kept without the written consent of the Landlord. A fine of R 500,00 applies to every breach.
Either party may cancel this lease by giving 30 days written notice.
Rental increases of up to 8 percent a year are permitted on renewal, with 60 days notice.
